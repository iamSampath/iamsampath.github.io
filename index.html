Passwordless Authentication WebAuthn / FIDO 2.0 · Google One-Tap · Apple Sign-In 130M+Active users

Drove eBay's passwordless authentication rollout across web and mobile for 130M+ active users — shipping WebAuthn biometric login, Google One-Tap, and Apple Sign-In. This made eBay one of the first major e-commerce platforms to enable biometrics as first-factor authentication on web browsers. Sign-in completion rates outperformed traditional passwords, especially on mobile.

The Google One-Tap integration alone increased sign-in completion 12% on mobile web and 5% on desktop, doubling overall sign-in volume across both platforms by removing authentication friction for returning users — directly improving conversion and session continuity for buyers and sellers.

Coordinated delivery across InfoSec, Privacy, Compliance, and Risk to ship features compliant with GDPR, CCPA, PSD2, and PCI-DSS across multiple jurisdictions, without slowing the release cadence.

About

The programs too large or too cross-functional for a conventional PM track.

I'm a Technical Program Manager with 10+ years delivering cloud infrastructure, identity systems, and GenAI platform programs at global e-commerce scale. My specialty is taking high-stakes, ambiguous initiatives — the ones that span six orgs and have no natural owner — and shipping them on schedule with outcomes you can measure.

At eBay, that has meant a zero-downtime GCP → AWS migration, passwordless authentication for 130M+ users, seller migration off PayPal, and a GenAI support program live in five countries. Underneath all of it sits the same delivery operating system: governance frameworks, risk registers, OKR dashboards, and escalation paths that cut decision lag from days to hours.

I work in both directions — fluent enough in cloud architecture, authentication protocols, and API integration to hold a design review, and comfortable enough in front of executives to run the steering committee that funds it. I was promoted at eBay from Project Lead through Technical Program Manager.
